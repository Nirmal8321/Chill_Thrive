Recovery Awareness

Understanding cold and heat therapy for better health and performance.

What is Cold Therapy?

Cold therapy involves exposing the body to cold temperatures to reduce inflammation, relieve muscle soreness, and enhance recovery. Ice baths are commonly used by athletes and wellness enthusiasts.

Science Behind Ice Baths

Cold exposure helps constrict blood vessels, reducing swelling and flushing out metabolic waste. After the session, blood flow increases, delivering oxygen and nutrients to muscles.

Heat vs Cold Therapy

Heat therapy relaxes muscles and improves circulation, while cold therapy reduces inflammation and pain. Using both together can provide complete recovery and relaxation benefits.

Who Should Avoid Ice Baths?

Individuals with heart conditions, high blood pressure, or respiratory issues should consult a medical professional before trying cold therapy.
